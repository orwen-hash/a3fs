How we work

Four steps. No surprises.

The sequence below is the actual order of every A3Fs project, so you always know what happens next.

STEP 01

Site walk & scope

We walk the site with you, document conditions, and confirm the scope in writing within 48 hours.

STEP 02

Fixed bid in 5 days

A line-item bid with unit pricing and a schedule. What we quote is what you pay, change orders only when scope changes.

STEP 03

Build & report

Certified crews, daily site logs, and a weekly photo report to your inbox. You never have to chase us for a status.

STEP 04

Punch list & handover

Joint walkthrough, punch list closed within 10 days, then warranties, as-builts, and lien waivers in one package.
